Cardstock — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 July 2026.

What this app does

Cardstock lets you keep a personal list of the credit cards you hold, so you can see their benefits (like lounge access or movie offers) in one place, and browse/compare other cards.

What data we collect

None, on any server. The list of cards you add to "My Wallet" is stored only on your own device, in your browser/app's local storage. We do not see it, collect it, or transmit it anywhere. Cardstock does not ask for your actual card number, CVV, expiry date, or any banking credentials — you're just picking cards from a built-in catalog to track.

Third parties

This app does not use analytics, ads, or third-party trackers, and does not share data with any third party, because it does not collect any data in the first place.

Data deletion

Uninstalling the app, or clearing the app's storage from your device settings, permanently deletes your saved wallet list. There is nothing stored elsewhere to delete.

Changes to this policy

If this app's data practices change in the future (for example, if account sync is added), this page will be updated to reflect that before the change ships.
